About ON Biotech

About Ostrich Nepal

ON biotech, established in 2019 has been a pioneer in commercial mushroom production in Nepal. Since full year operations from FY 2077/78  the company has shown impressive growth. From producing 473,817 kg of mushrooms in FY 77/78, ON Biotech has increased its production to over 1.13 million kg by FY 2080/81.In total, the company has produced around 3.28 million kilograms of mushrooms by the end of FY 2080/81. This growth reflects continuous progress in farming technology, better infrastructure, and high-quality spawn and compost production.

ON Biotech operates 58 growing rooms fitted with modern equipment and technology. These rooms help control temperature, humidity, and airflow to create the best environment for growing mushrooms all year round. This technology helps increase production, maintain quality, and make the process more efficient and reliable.

Currently, there are around 250 employees working directly in the company. In addition, many local farmers are also involved indirectly, supporting different parts of the production process. We highly value their efforts and dedication in helping build a stronger and more sustainable mushroom industry in Nepal.  

How it started

In the heart of the Himalayas, amidst the towering peaks and verdant valleys, a visionary named Tenzing Norgay embarked on an extraordinary journey. As a farmer, Tenzing recognized the untapped potential of Nepal’s fertile lands for mushroom cultivation, envisioning a future where sustainable agriculture could transform lives and enrich the environment. With unwavering determination, he gathered a small but passionate team of local farmers, united by a shared dream of producing world-class mushrooms using environmentally friendly methods. Thus, “Ostrich Nepal” was born, fueled by a bold mission to revolutionize the industry and uplift communities.

Starting with a humble plot in the Kathmandu Valley, Tenzing and his team ingeniously blended time-honored farming practices with cutting-edge technology, creating a harmonious synergy between tradition and innovation. They meticulously nurtured each mushroom, ensuring the highest standards of quality and sustainability. As the years passed, Ostrich Nepal flourished, earning acclaim as a leader in the industry and a beacon of hope for local farmers. The company became renowned for its unwavering dedication to ecological sustainability, community development, and the empowerment of women in agriculture.

Our story is a testament to the transformative power of passion, resilience, and an enduring belief in the potential of sustainable agriculture to uplift lives and enrich the environment. Today, Ostrich Nepal continues to innovate, setting new standards for excellence and inspiring a new generation of farmers to embrace sustainable practices and create a brighter future for Nepal.

Mission

To meet Nepal’s growing demand for fresh, high-quality mushrooms through reliable, large-scale production practices ensuring year-round cultivation.

Vision

To lead Nepal towards self-reliance in agri production by ensuring consistent supply & creating lasting opportunities for communities nationwide.

Goals
  • To implement eco friendly practices by utilizing locally available resources for production 
  • To strengthen our presence in the domestic market contributing to the economic development of Nepal. 
  • To continuously improve production technique and yield
  • To provide locally grown food at every nepali home.
