Scaling Mushroom Industry Sustainably

Scaling
Mushroom
Industry Sustainably

ON Biotech, established in 2019 has been at the forefront of Nepal’s mushroom industry. Driving the production of high quality spawn, fresh mushroom and other mushroom based products, our mission at ON Biotech is to make Nepal self-reliant on mushroom production through technological innovation, sustainability and local collaboration.

State-of-the-art facilities meeting growing demand

As pioneers of commercial mushroom farming in Nepal, we combine modern technology with growing expertise to harvest fresh, nutrition rich quality mushrooms. Behind every harvest lies a dedicated team of over 250 professionals, who are further strengthened by a strong network of more than 1000 local farmers, suppliers and wholesalers. Together we are not only producing mushrooms but also creating employment, supporting rural livelihoods and contributing to Nation building through sustainable development of Nepal’s agricultural sector.

Mission

To meet Nepal’s growing demand for fresh, high-quality mushrooms through reliable, large-scale production practices ensuring year-round cultivation.

Vision

To lead Nepal towards self-reliance in agri production by ensuring consistent supply & creating lasting opportunities for communities nationwide.

Goals
  • To implement eco friendly practices by utilizing locally available resources for production 
  • To strengthen our presence in the domestic market contributing to the economic development of Nepal. 
  • To continuously improve production technique and yield
  • o provide locally grown food at every nepali home.
